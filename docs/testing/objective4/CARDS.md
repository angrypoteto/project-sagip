# Objective 4 trial cards

The facts the responder reports, the same for every method. All names are
made up. Read the card, then file it three ways, in this order:

1. **Current way:** the baseline form replica (`baseline-form/index.html`,
   MDRRMD's current form rebuilt from its PDF templates), timed from
   **Start** to **Submit**.
2. **S.A.G.I.P. by hand:** F6, from **Complete rescue** to **Report sent**,
   tapping the answers.
3. **S.A.G.I.P. with Fill from notes:** F6 again, writing short notes and
   tapping **Fill from notes**, then checking.

For 2 and 3 the incident is assigned and marked on scene first (not timed);
the place, date, time, district, barangay, month and quarter are filled in
by S.A.G.I.P. For 1 the responder enters them from the card. Photos: the
three TEST PHOTO images in the phone's gallery.
