# Objective 4 timed trial: steps

The comparison Chapter 3 calls for: the time an administrator takes to
prepare the same post-disaster report **by hand** and **with S.A.G.I.P.**

**Reduction = (T_by_hand − T_with_system) / T_by_hand × 100.** Target: 30% or
more.

## Who and what

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Participant** | An MDRRMD administrator who prepares these reports (best), or a team member acting as one. Use **different people** for the two methods, or the same person with the hand method **first**, so knowing the figures does not speed up the second |
| **Periods** | Three report periods, for example the last 7 days, the last 30 days, and one storm week. The same periods for both methods |
| **Timer** | A phone stopwatch; one team member keeps time and fills in `timed_trial_tally.csv` |
| **The finished report** | The same contents either way: the six sections (situation overview, incidents, affected population, damage, response, remarks) with their figures |

## Method 1: by hand (MDRRMD's current way)

1. Start the timer when the participant begins.
2. They gather the figures the way they do today (incident log, the
   responders' reports, Excel, the hotline log) and write the report in
   Word using MDRRMD's usual format.
3. Stop the timer when the report is ready to send.
4. Note what they used and anything that held them up in the remarks.

## Method 2: with S.A.G.I.P.

1. Start the timer when the participant opens **MDRRMD reports → New report**.
2. They choose the period, press **Collect records**, then **Rewrite with the
   language model**, read every section against the figures, write
   section 6 (remarks), and correct anything wrong.
3. Stop the timer when they press **Mark as final** (or **Download PDF**).
4. Count how many sentences they had to change in the remarks column.
